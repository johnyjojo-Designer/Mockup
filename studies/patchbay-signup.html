Style notes for the real brief

  • OutlineEvery object gets a 2.5px ink stroke. Objects meet the ground through outline and colour, never through blur.
  • DepthFlat-front extrusion: a 5–14px side face in a darker shade of the same hue, a light bevel on the top edge, and a 7px diagonal cast shadow with no blur.
  • ScreensDark recesses with a 5px hard inner wall and one diagonal glare. Text inside is light; LCD readouts use lime.
  • ControlsKeys and switches press down into their side face. LEDs report each field: amber while editing, green when valid, red when it needs a fix.
  • ColourOne saturated hue per object, placed side by side and never blended. Bone and smoke act as neutrals between them.
  • TypeArchivo condensed 850–900 for plates and keys, Archivo for typed text, and DM Mono caps for labels and readouts.
